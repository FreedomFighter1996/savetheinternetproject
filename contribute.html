Contribute

Four ways in. Pick the one that fits your week.

There is no membership, no application and no vetting. The only real requirement is that you finish something small rather than plan something large.

Path 01

Write

Essays, guides, translations, plain-language explainers. The single most useful thing right now is a walkthrough that a person with no technical background can follow to the end without getting stuck.

Time: 2 hours and up

Path 02

Build

Tools that make owning your own corner of the web less painful. Import scripts, archive helpers, templates, anything that removes one step from the process. Small and finished beats ambitious and abandoned.

Time: a weekend

Path 03

Document

Report what you have watched break. A platform that closed, an archive that vanished, a policy that quietly changed. Include dates and sources. This is the least glamorous work here and probably the most valuable.

Time: 20 minutes

Path 04

Spread

Send the pitch to exactly one person who would argue with it. Not a broadcast, not a repost. One person, chosen deliberately, who will push back and make the argument stronger.

Time: 5 minutes

Start now

Three things you can finish today.

Each of these takes under an hour, costs nothing or nearly nothing, and leaves you better off even if this project goes nowhere.

  1. Register a domain in your own name

    Roughly the price of two coffees a year. It does not need a site behind it yet. It needs to exist and to be yours, because everything else becomes possible afterwards.

  2. Export your own archive from one platform

    Pick the account holding the work you would most hate to lose. Request the export. Note how long it took and what came back broken, then send us that note.

  3. Archive three links you care about

    Anything you have cited, saved or would want to reread in ten years. Put copies somewhere you control. Assume the original will be gone, because statistically it will.

Stay in the loop

One email when there is something worth reading.

Not a newsletter with a schedule to fill. It goes out when the log contains something that actually matters, which will sometimes mean nothing for weeks.

Not wired up yet. This form is a placeholder. Connect it to Netlify Forms, Formspree or your mail provider before launch. Instructions are in the HTML comment directly above it.

About money

We are not asking for any.

Not out of principle theatre. Taking donations means accounting, obligations and a reporting burden that would consume more time than it buys at this size. If that changes, it will be announced in the daily log with the reasoning attached, before anything is collected.

If you want to spend money usefully today, spend it on your own domain and your own backups.
