Day 001 2 Sep 2026

Starting in public, on purpose

First day. The site exists: five pages, no tracking, no build step, no framework. It is deliberately plain files, because a project arguing that the web should be durable and ownable should not itself depend on a toolchain that will be broken in three years.

The temptation today was to plan. Write a roadmap, sketch the tool, design a logo, decide on a governance model for a project with one person in it. All of that is procrastination wearing a serious face. The rule going forward is that a day counts only if something shipped or something was learned, and writing a plan is neither.

What is still missing, honestly: every statistic on the home page is a placeholder, the contribute form is not connected to anything, and the about page does not yet say who is writing this. Those are the next three jobs, in that order.

One open question that will decide a lot: is the useful output here documentation or tooling? Writing is faster and reaches more people. Tools are slower and actually change what someone can do on a Tuesday afternoon. Leaning towards tools, with the documentation as the thing that makes the tools usable, but that is not settled.
